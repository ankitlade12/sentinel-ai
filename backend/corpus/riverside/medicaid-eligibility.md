# Medicaid Eligibility Basics

Medicaid provides health coverage to eligible low-income people. Eligibility is
based mainly on **Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI)** for most applicants,
along with household size, and — for some pathways — age, disability, or
pregnancy.

## Income-based (MAGI) eligibility

For most children, pregnant people, parents, and adults in states that expanded
Medicaid, eligibility is determined by MAGI as a percentage of the federal
poverty level. The exact percentage thresholds differ by state and by group
(for example, children are often eligible at higher income levels than adults).

## Non-MAGI pathways

People who are 65 or older, blind, or have a disability may qualify through
non-MAGI pathways that also consider assets. Long-term-care eligibility has
additional rules.

## Immigration status

Eligibility can depend on immigration status. Some lawfully present immigrants
are eligible; others face waiting periods. Emergency Medicaid may cover
emergency care regardless of immigration status for those who otherwise meet
eligibility. Because these rules are detailed and state-specific, confirm
current state thresholds and immigrant-eligibility rules before advising.
