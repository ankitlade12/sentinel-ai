# Riverside Community Legal Aid — Frequently Asked Questions

## Office hours

The clinic is open **Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.** We are
closed on weekends and federal holidays. Walk-in intake hours are **Tuesday and
Thursday, 10:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.**

## How to get help

- **Phone intake:** call the clinic during office hours to be screened for
  eligibility and scheduled with an advocate.
- **Walk-in:** come during walk-in intake hours; please arrive early, as
  capacity is limited.
- **Online:** submit the intake form on our website and a staff member will
  follow up within two business days.

## Cost

Our services are **free** for clients who meet our income and case-type
eligibility guidelines. We never charge for a consultation. We do not collect
government filing fees on your behalf; those are paid directly to the relevant
agency.

## Languages

Intake is available in **English and Spanish**. For other languages, we arrange
interpretation; please tell us your preferred language when you contact us.

## What to bring

Bring any documents related to your issue — notices, letters, leases, agency
correspondence, and identification. Do not worry if you are missing something;
an advocate will tell you what is needed.

## What we handle

We assist with immigration, housing, and public-benefits matters. If your issue
is outside our areas, we will try to refer you to another organization.
