# Employment Authorization Document (EAD), Form I-765

Many noncitizens must request employment authorization before working in the
United States by filing Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization.
Approved applicants receive an Employment Authorization Document (EAD).

## Who needs to file

Whether you need an EAD depends on your immigration category. Some categories
are authorized to work incident to status and do not file Form I-765; others
must apply and wait for approval before starting work.

## Renewals and automatic extensions

File to renew an EAD before it expires. For certain eligible categories, filing
a timely renewal application can provide an **automatic extension** of the prior
EAD for a limited period while the renewal is pending. The length of the
automatic extension and the categories eligible for it are set by regulation and
have changed over time — confirm the current extension period on the official
USCIS Form I-765 page rather than relying on a remembered number.

## Fees

A filing fee may apply depending on category, and fee waivers (Form I-912) may
be available. Confirm the current fee and any waiver eligibility on the USCIS
site before filing.
