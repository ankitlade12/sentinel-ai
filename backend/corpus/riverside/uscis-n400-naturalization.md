# Form N-400, Application for Naturalization

Form N-400 is the application to become a naturalized U.S. citizen. Most
applicants must first be lawful permanent residents (Green Card holders) who
meet continuous-residence and physical-presence requirements.

## Continuous residence

- General rule: at least **5 years** as a permanent resident before applying.
- Married to and living with a U.S. citizen: at least **3 years**, if the spouse
  has been a citizen for that entire period.

You may file Form N-400 up to **90 days before** you reach the 5-year (or 3-year)
continuous-residence requirement. This 90-day early-filing window applies to the
residence requirement only; you must still meet all other eligibility criteria.

## Other core requirements

- Be at least 18 years old at the time of filing.
- Demonstrate continuous residence and physical presence in the United States.
- Show good moral character.
- Pass an English and a civics test (with limited age/residence exemptions).
- Take the Oath of Allegiance.

## Fees and fee waivers

The filing fee and biometric services fee are listed on the official USCIS
Form N-400 page. Fee waivers (Form I-912) and reduced fees may be available for
applicants who meet income guidelines. Always confirm the current fee on the
USCIS site before filing.
